The user dislikes generic art and noise; wants art "a player would pay for", clean, with its own style.

tilted (near lanes low), horses in profile, rail in front, crowd + city skyline with parallax, mini-oval
in the HUD, bends that visibly curve. Ambience is "very important".

**Why:** stated in the 2026-10-15 brief for the race concept (design/race_concept.png + .md).
**How to apply:** for race art, keep crowds/windows as calm periodic textures (no per-pixel random),
tile-aligned so counts stay low; deliver concept PNGs at 1x and 3x with tile and sprites-per-line
counts. Approval status of the concept: check design/race_concept.md / GDD before building assets.
